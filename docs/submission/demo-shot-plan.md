## Capture rules

- Use a 1920×1080 canvas and keep all important text readable at normal playback speed.
- Show one control change per visual action; do not jump multiple filters or stages in one cut.
- Keep the cursor inside the final crop and derive click points from live controls.
- Do not expose cookies, OTPs, raw email addresses, confirmation material, database URLs, provider identifiers, terminal output, or source payloads.
- Keep the mailbox code entry out of frame; show the exact alert review before it and the activation result after it.
- Narrate the distinction between live WebMCP tools and Jobbbler's durable alert worker.
- Describe the request-bound interaction receipt as evidence of the exact action and context, not cryptographic identity.
- Never invoke `decide_application_submission` in this cut; the trust-focused ending is the exact pending decision on the visible owner review surface, not a receipt or submission claim.
- Record a clean production run first; use post-production only for pacing, crops, cursor treatment, captions, and audio.
